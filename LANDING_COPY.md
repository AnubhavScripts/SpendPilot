## ❓ FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

**1. Do I need to connect my credit card or bank account?**
No. SpendPilot is completely self-serve and deterministic. You simply input the tools and plans you are currently paying for, and our engine instantly calculates your potential savings.

**2. How does SpendPilot know what tools are redundant?**
We map the capabilities of the top 50 AI enterprise tools. For example, if you are paying for GitHub Copilot but also paying for Cursor Pro (which has native AI autocompletion built-in), our engine flags that as a redundancy based on your primary use case.

**3. Is my data stored securely?**
Yes. Your audit is generated entirely in your browser using local logic. If you choose to save your audit or share it with your team, the anonymous breakdown is stored securely in our Postgres database with strict Row Level Security. 

**4. What if my specific AI tool isn't listed?**
Our database covers the major foundational models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini), coding assistants (Copilot, Cursor), and creative tools (Midjourney). We update our pricing and feature matrices weekly as new tools hit the market.

**5. How much does SpendPilot cost?**
The audit is 100% free. If you want us to handle the actual cancellation, vendor negotiation, and license consolidation on your behalf, we charge a flat 15% fee on the first year's generated savings.
